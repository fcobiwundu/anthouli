HOW IT WORKS

From accounting profit to taxable profit.

Corporation tax is generally calculated from taxable profits rather than simply applying the tax rate to accounting profit.

01

Start with accounting profit

Begin with the company's profit before corporation tax as shown in the accounts.

02

Apply tax adjustments

Add back non-deductible expenses and deduct applicable exempt income, capital allowances and other eligible deductions.

03

Apply the tax rate

The resulting estimated taxable profit is multiplied by the applicable corporation tax rate.

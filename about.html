THE FIRM

A professional firm with a practical perspective.

C. Anthouli Ltd is a privately held financial services firm based in Larnaca, Cyprus, with a focus on tax, audit and advisory.

The firm's public professional profile reflects a practical approach to professional services, with published guidance covering taxation, accounting, audit preparation, company structures, tax residency and business compliance in Cyprus.

The emphasis is not simply on completing a filing or meeting a deadline. It is on helping clients understand their financial position, the obligations that apply to them and the implications of the decisions they are making.

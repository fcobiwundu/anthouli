What services does C. Anthouli provide?

C. Anthouli provides assurance, tax advisory and tax compliance services, together with accounting and business support for clients operating in Cyprus and internationally.

Who does C. Anthouli work with?

The firm supports businesses, entrepreneurs and individuals with accounting, taxation, compliance and related financial matters.

How do I know whether I should operate as a sole trader or through a company?

The right structure depends on your activities, income, responsibilities, future plans and compliance obligations. Professional advice can help you understand the differences before making the decision.

What are the main tax obligations for a business in Cyprus?

Obligations vary depending on the structure and activities of the business. They may include accounting records, tax declarations, filings and other compliance requirements.

Do you assist with international or cross-border matters?

C. Anthouli's positioning includes support for national and international corporations, including matters where tax residency, foreign income or cross-border considerations are relevant.
